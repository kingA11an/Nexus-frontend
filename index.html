We don't just sell equipment. We build environments.

We work with families and institutions to transform existing spaces into environments that support sensory regulation, learning, communication, therapy, participation and independence.

1

Assess

Needs, site and user requirements, sensory considerations.

2

Design

3

Equip

Sensory, therapy, learning and adaptive resources sourced and supplied.

4

Set up

Delivery, installation, positioning and safety checks.

5

Orient

User orientation for staff, caregivers and learners.

6

Support

Ongoing guidance so the space keeps working for its users.
